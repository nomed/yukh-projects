# Support

## Questions and reproducible problems

Use the repository's structured issue forms. Provide the smallest reproduction possible and use only synthetic repositories, projects, field values, logs, and screenshots.

Maintainers may close or redact reports that expose private context or cannot be discussed safely in public. Reproduce the generic behavior before resubmitting.

## Security and privacy

Vulnerabilities and suspected data disclosures must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never a public issue.

## Scope

Community support covers public Yukh Projects behavior and documentation. Maintainers cannot diagnose private deployments from production data in the public repository.
