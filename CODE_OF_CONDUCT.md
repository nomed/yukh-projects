# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Yukh Projects a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical preference.

We act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give and accept constructive, evidence-based feedback.
- Focus discussion on the reusable problem and project principles.
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and follow the consumer neutrality policy.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to `@nomed` through a private channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or temporarily or permanently restrict contributions and participation when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and disclosed only when doing so is safe and appropriate.
